About us

Practical engineering for a cleaner grid.

Northvane started as a small consultancy of grid engineers. Today we deliver and operate renewable projects end to end, with the same focus on getting the details right.

What we value

Four habits we hire for

Own the outcome

We stay with a project long after handover, so we design for the decades it will run.

Show the working

Assumptions, models and risks are written down and shared, not kept in one person's head.

Safety first, always

Anyone on site can stop work. Nobody is ever asked to trade safety for schedule.

Learn in the field

Engineers spend time at operating sites, and what they see changes the next design.
